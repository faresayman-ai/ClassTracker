## 🚀 What is ClassTracker?

**ClassTracker** is a student productivity web app that automatically syncs with **Google Classroom** to collect all your assignments and quiz deadlines in one clean, organized place.

No more checking Classroom every 5 minutes. No more missed due dates.

---

## ✨ Features

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%">

### 🔄 Google Classroom Sync
Automatically scans all your Classroom courses and pulls every assignment and quiz deadline into a unified checklist — updated daily.

</td>
<td width="50%">

### 📅 Smart Calendar Integration
Deadlines are pushed to your **Google Calendar** as events with layered reminders at **6 days · 3 days · 1 day · 6 hrs · 1 hr** before due.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%">

### 🍎 Apple Calendar (iOS)
No Google Calendar? No problem. On iOS, ClassTracker connects to your **native Apple Calendar** automatically.

</td>
<td width="50%">

### 💬 Live Study Rooms
Real-time chat rooms with classmates — join with a 6-character code, collaborate, and stay focused together.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%">

### 📱 Progressive Web App (PWA)
Install directly from your browser on **Android, iOS, or desktop** — no App Store needed. Works offline with cached data.

</td>
<td width="50%">

### ⚡ Focus Mode & Deadline Alerts
Visual indicators for what's due soon, smart reminders, and a ✅ checklist to track progress.

</td>
</tr>
</table>

## 🔗 Try It

**👉 [classtracker-a8ea3.web.app](https://classtracker-a8ea3.web.app/)**

1. Sign in with your Google account  
2. Grant read-only access to Google Classroom  
3. Your deadlines sync automatically  
4. Optionally connect Google or Apple Calendar for reminders  
5. Join or create a study room with classmates  

### Installing as a PWA

| Platform | Steps |
|---|---|
| **Android / Chrome** | Tap the install icon in the address bar → *Add to Home Screen* |
| **iOS / Safari** | Tap Share → *Add to Home Screen* |
| **Desktop / Chrome** | Click the install icon in the browser address bar |
